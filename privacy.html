⛈️ StormPark Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

StormPark is an independent student project that shows live parking availability on campus. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by St. John's University. This page explains what information StormPark collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Google account basics: your name, email address, and profile photo, used only to sign you in. They are stored by Firebase Authentication (a Google service).
  • Your current check-in: which lot you checked into and when. It is deleted as soon as you check out.

What we don't collect

  • We do not track your location. The app only knows a lot if you tap "I parked here."
  • We do not keep a history of where you've parked.
  • We do not sell, share, or use your data for ads.

Who can see your information

  • Other users see only lot counts, never who is parked where.
  • Your check-in can be read only by you.
  • The project owner can see the list of signed-in accounts (name and email) in Firebase, but only to run and secure the app.

Services we use

  • Firebase (Google) for sign-in and the database
  • reCAPTCHA (Google) to block bots. It may collect device and browser information under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel to host the website, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses
  • OpenStreetMap for map images

Deleting your data

Sign in and tap "Delete my data" at the bottom of the map. This checks you out and permanently deletes your StormPark account.
